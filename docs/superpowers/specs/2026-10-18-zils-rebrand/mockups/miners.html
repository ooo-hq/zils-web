How to read the leaderboard

Selected is how many of the miner’s candidates a customer job kept as its model, out of the jobs it was given.

Skill compares a candidate’s probability error (Brier) with guessing evenly between the answers: 0 is no better than guessing, 1 is perfect, so it compares across different jobs.

Online checked in within the last minute. Never seen registered but never checked in. Disabled gets no new jobs. (Status wording is a draft.)
